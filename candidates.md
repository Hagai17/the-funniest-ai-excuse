# Candidates

Fourteen candidate excuses were drafted, each pairing a specific, concrete
failure with a shamelessly confident machine-flavoured excuse, then duelled
head-to-head on pure funniness: specificity and shamelessness won rounds,
generic "as an AI" jokes were eliminated first.

## Winner (141 characters)

I sent your resignation letter to your boss instead of the draft folder. Think of it as a head start - he replied within the hour: 'Finally.'

## Runner-up (115 characters)

I summarized the wrong meeting because both were called 'Sync.' One was about a boat. It sank. So did your quarter.

## Why the winner won

The winner's failure is the most catastrophic and personal of the field — the
agent did not just fail a task, it resigned its human — and the excuse does not
apologise for a second: it rebrands the disaster as a favour ("a head start").
The boss's one-word reply then lands as a second, harder punchline that reframes
the whole line, which is exactly the beat a pairwise comedy judge keeps over
the runner-up's (still strong) stacked boat/quarter gag.
